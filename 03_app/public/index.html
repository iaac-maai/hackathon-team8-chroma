Walk mode

Walk the street

Point the camera at the street (or at yourself) and walk. Every few seconds Chroma reads the place, the light and the colours; the music changes with them, and your steps set the tempo.

You will be asked for the camera, motion and location. The video is never recorded: only each view's colours and labels are saved with your walk.
