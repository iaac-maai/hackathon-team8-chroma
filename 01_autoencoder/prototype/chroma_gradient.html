Chroma gradient maps

Drop a photo of a place and see its gradient map: the 8 colours it has from the deepest shadows to the brightest highlights, the photo repainted through them, and the music values the team derives from them. Everything runs in your browser; photos are not uploaded.

  1. Sort every pixel from darkest to lightest
  2. Cut them into 8 equal groups
  3. Average colour of each group = one stop
  4. Repaint each pixel with the stop at its lightness
Team 8 · AI Methods hackathon, IAAC. Same method as data_prep/extract_gradient.py and extract_palette.py in the team repository: photos are centre-cropped and reduced to 128 × 128 px, colours are averaged in CIE Lab. Scale rule: warm and bright → D lydian, bright → D major, dark → D minor, in between → D dorian.
